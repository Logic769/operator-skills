# Sample rubric

Invented example only. The weights below sum to 100.

| Criterion | Weight | Full points when | Zero when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Buyer title | 30 | Head, VP, Director or Founder in a relevant function | Intern, student, unrelated function |
| Company size | 25 | 20 to 500 employees | Under 5 or over 5,000 |
| Industry | 20 | Design, dev or data agency, consultancy, recruiting firm | Government, education |
| Region | 10 | Target regions | Outside service area |
| Signal | 15 | A dated buying signal from the last 90 days | No evidence |

Tiers: A = 75 to 100, B = 50 to 74, C = 25 to 49, D = under 25.
